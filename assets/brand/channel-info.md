## Descrição do canal (copiar e colar em Personalização → Informações básicas)

Before the cameras, there were the records.

Gaslight Archive tells the true stories behind history's forgotten tragedies — the disasters, mysteries and dark chapters from the age of candlelight and gas lamps, when the only witnesses were engravings, newspapers and court ledgers.

Every story is researched from primary sources and told with respect for the people who lived it. No sensationalism. Just what really happened, why it happened, and what it changed.

New documentaries every week.
